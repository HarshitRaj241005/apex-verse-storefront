How to use Career OS
1
Applications
Track roles, stages, priority and next action. Edit records anytime; linked application follow-ups stay synchronized.
2
Interviews + Networking
Link interviews to applications and use relationship follow-ups for the people who can move your search forward.
3
Offers
Attach offers to applications, compare up to three, and review the structured decision score.
4
Backup before upgrades
Export JSON before installing a newer ZIP. Restore it in the new version to carry your dataset forward.
Storage model
Local-first
No account, server or subscription is required for this prototype.
Browser-bound
Clearing browser storage or switching devices does not transfer your records automatically.
Portable
JSON backup is the source-of-truth transfer format between versions.
